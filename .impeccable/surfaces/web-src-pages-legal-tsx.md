Scope: Public Terms and Privacy routes. Mode: Read.

Audience: prospective and current Flurry account holders. Job: understand the
rules of using the service and how account, repository, operational, mail, and
billing data are handled. Primary action: move between the two policies or
contact Flurry with a question. Constraints: inherit the existing Flurry shell,
remain readable on mobile, avoid invented corporate or jurisdictional claims,
and describe only behavior supported by the product.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet reference document that is easy to scan and cite. It refuses a
marketing hero and keeps the policy itself as the dominant object.

OWN-WORLD: Flurry's existing white and soft-blue system, compact sans-serif
type, fine neutral rules, blue links, restrained square corners, and no new
decorative vocabulary.

STORY: The reader sees the effective date and plain-language summary, scans a
stable section index, reads short sections in sequence, and can reach the other
policy or contact address without leaving the shell.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The policy title and summary occupy the left reading column;
the section index begins beside it on desktop and precedes the text on mobile.
The first substantive section remains visible without an oversized masthead.

FORM: Direct scoped extension of the established Flurry world, code-led Read
surface. Seed key: scoped-legal-extension.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: The public paid offer remains unpublished, so billing language must
describe the mechanism without promising a specific price or refund program.
